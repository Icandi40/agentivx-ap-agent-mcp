How it works

Every invoice takes the same path, and every step is logged.

1
Invoice arrivesPDF or email text
2
Agent reads the fieldsvendor, invoice number, dates, PO, lines, total
3
Three-way matchinvoice vs purchase order vs receiving log
4
Agent drafts a proposalnothing is written to QuickBooks yet
5
A person approves or rejectswith their name on the decision
6
The approved bill posts to QuickBooksafter every check runs again

The design choice

Review page and CLI

a separate program, for people
approve
reject

post_approved_proposal refuses anything a person has not approved. Approve and reject do not exist on the agent's side.
